# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Longstraw Carbon ("Longstraw", "we", "us") handles information when you use our secure file sharing and data room service (the "Service").

##1. Information we collect
- Account information: name and email address when you sign in or verify an upload.
- Content: files and documents you or your organization upload or share through the Service.
- Usage data: view sessions, link interactions, and basic telemetry used to operate and improve the Service (for example analytics events sent to our measurement endpoints).
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, and request logs used for security, abuse prevention, and debugging.

##2. How we use information
- To provide, secure, and operate the Service;
- To deliver shared content to its intended recipients;
- To verify identity for protected uploads and data rooms;
- To monitor performance, prevent abuse, and fix errors.

##3. Sharing and disclosure
- Content you share is visible to the recipients of that share link or data room, as configured by the sender.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Longstraw, our users, or third parties.

##4. Retention
Account and content data is retained for as long as the account or share link is active, or as needed to provide the Service. Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and debugging, then deleted or aggregated.

##5. Security
We use encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege practices to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we work to promptly address any incident that affects your data.

##6. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact us at tech@longstraw.in and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

##7. International transfers
Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

##8. Children
The Service is built for organizational use and is not directed at children under 16.

##9. Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date.

##10. Contact
Questions about privacy: tech@longstraw.in.
